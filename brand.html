About ESEN’CIA

We begin with the skin.

Healthy-looking skin is achieved through balance, not excess. ESEN’CIA combines Korean skincare science with purposeful bio-active ingredients and comfortable daily formulations.

Brand Story

Essential Science for Healthy Skin

Skincare begins with one simple idea: listen to what the skin truly needs. In a world of fast-moving trends and excess, ESEN’CIA chooses understanding, balance and consistency.

Advanced Korean skincare science is paired with clinically selected bio-active ingredients and focused formulation design to address specific concerns while respecting the skin barrier.

Concern-Led Skin Solution

There is no single answer for every skin type.

Dryness, sensitivity, dullness, excess sebum and loss of firmness each require a different approach. Every formula begins by understanding the skin concern before selecting the right actives and technology.
